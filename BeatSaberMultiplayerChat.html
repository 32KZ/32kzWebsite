Beat Saber Multiplayer Chat

  • Beat Saber
  • Fork

A fork of Roy de Jong's Beat Saber Multiplayer Chat, which adds voice and text chat to multiplayer lobbies. I forked it in June 2023 while working on Beat Saber mods of my own.

All credit for the mod goes to its original author.

What it does

  • Text chat in the lobby with chat bubbles and notification sounds.
  • Voice chat in lobbies and during play.
  • Mute buttons that remember muted players.
