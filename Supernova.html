SUPERNOVA

  • Electron
  • Windows
  • Private

SUPERNOVA is the successor to ZEKE-UI: a standalone desktop command centre for Windows. Every widget is its own managed native window, grouped into workspaces that remember their layout, project folder and state.

Press Shift+Alt+Space anywhere and a Raycast-style panel opens on top of everything, giving quick access to the assistant and the rest of SUPERNOVA.

What it does

  • Widgets for Files, Terminal, Code, Assistant, Launcher, Tasks, CPU, GPU, System, Global Situation, Clock and more.
  • Workspaces keep their layouts and widget state while you hide or switch between them.
  • Widget dial: a semicircular picker you can scroll, rotate with the arrow keys or pick from with 1 to 7.
  • Overview (Shift+Alt+O) draws your monitors in their real arrangement, with drag-and-drop to move widgets between workspaces.
  • Its own command-line launcher and a Windows installer.
