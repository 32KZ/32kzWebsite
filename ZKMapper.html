ZKMapper

  • C#
  • .NET
  • Automation

ZKMapper is a for-fun automation project. Give it a company name and some search filters and it works through the company's LinkedIn people page, collecting everyone who matches into a CSV.

It runs as an interactive command-line app and supports several companies in one go.

What it does

  • Filters by job title keyword and country or region.
  • Profile extraction of name, headline, current roles and profile link.
  • Email pattern guesses based on name and company domain.
  • Saved browser sessions, so it doesn't need to log in every run.
