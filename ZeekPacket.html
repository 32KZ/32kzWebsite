ZEEK-PACKET

  • Minecraft
  • Meteor add-on
  • Private

ZEEK-PACKET is a private add-on for Meteor Client on Minecraft 1.21. It brings together tools from other mods, such as Autism Client and UI-Utils, alongside modules I've written myself.

It's designed to be verbose and easy to use, with chat output telling you exactly what each module is doing.

What it does

  • Packet delay modules, basic and advanced, with in-game notifications when packets are held.
  • Add latency to everything sent to and from the server, for testing.
  • UI-Utils port updated for 1.21.11. Full credit to its original authors.
  • ZKPanic: one switch that disables and locks every module, clears keybinds and hides the client watermark.
