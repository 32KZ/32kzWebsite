ZKMapper

Maps the people at a company on LinkedIn and exports them as a structured CSV.

Status
Public repository
Language
C#
Started
2026

ZKMapper automates company research on LinkedIn. Give it one or more company names and some search filters, and it collects the people associated with each company into a CSV.

It's built in C# with Playwright for browser automation and Spectre.Console for an interactive command-line interface. It's a for-fun project, not intended for commercial use.

View on GitHubAll projects

Highlights

  • Company people search with job title and region filters
  • Automatic scrolling and profile discovery
  • Likely work email generation from name patterns
  • CSV export of the results
